# AI Output Field Guide

Approved positioning (2026-10-25): An interactive field guide to structuring knowledge that people can understand and AI agents can use.

Anuj approved the implementation plan on 2026-10-24. This is a free, static,
AI-focused field guide that also works for people doing the work themselves.
Visitors explore approaches, drill into their principles, and apply them through
original examples and portable templates. The launch audience is broad knowledge
workers, with prerequisites explained on advanced entries.

Launch: 30 complete approaches, six task guides, three interactive cases, six
comparisons, and three drills per approach. Each entry explains history, purpose,
mechanism, benefits, limits, use cases, sources, and AI applications. No model API,
backend, user account, database, analytics service, or external runtime assets.
Bookmarks and exercise activity are stored on the visitor's browser only.

Publish as a separate public GitHub Pages repository at
https://tech.anujsadani.in/ai-output-field-guide/. Add a short catalog listing to
the tech site and a persistent description/link to anujsadani.in. No extra article.
Original code is MIT; original educational content is CC BY 4.0. Third-party
standards and referenced publications retain their rights.

Success is finding a suitable approach, explaining why it fits, adapting an
artifact, and recognizing its limitations. Counts and copied prompts alone do
not establish learning. Five-reader task validation is outstanding until actual
participants complete it; browser tests do not substitute for that study.
